## Community Contributions

To maintain a clear licensing structure and facilitate community contributions, all contributions
must be licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) to be
accepted.

By submitting a contribution, you agree to this licensing.

This approach avoids the need for a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) while ensuring clarity
regarding license terms. We will only accept contributions licensed under Apache 2.0. We require
Apache 2.0 for contributions specifically because of its explicit patent license provisions, which
help protect the project and its users.
